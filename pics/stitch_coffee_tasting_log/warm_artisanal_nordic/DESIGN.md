## Brand & Style

This design system channels the quiet sophistication of a modern Copenhagen roastery combined with the intimate tactile precision of an analog field ledger. The core audience comprises dedicated home baristas, specialty roasters, and sensory enthusiasts who value exactitude alongside ritualistic warmth.

The design movement blends **Tactile Modernism** with **Scandinavian Warm Minimalism**:
- **Organic Precision:** Highly legible operational telemetry (grind size, water temperature, flow rates, extraction ratios) paired with sensory editorial pacing.
- **Physicality via Micro-Details:** Delicate hairline separations mimicking high-grade paper debossing, velvety cream surfaces, and intentional negative space that slows the user into an observant tasting mindset.
- **Understated Restraint:** Visual impact stems from typography scale contrasts and material-like card finishes rather than synthetic gradients or gratuitous drop-shadows.

## Colors

The palette takes direct cue from coffee alchemy and natural ceramic surfaces, configured in a default warm light mode.

- **Primary (`#2C1810` - Deep Roasted Espresso):** Anchor tone used for authoritative titles, high-emphasis icons, and structural focal points.
- **Secondary (`#D97736` - Warm Amber Caramel):** Active accent for brew timers, critical CTAs, dial-in highlights, and extraction peak states.
- **Tertiary (`#6E8B68` - Fresh Matcha Sage):** Botanical secondary accent reserved for origin/terroir tags, natural processing flags, wash methods, and successful dial-in status indicators.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF8F5` - Oat Canvas & `#F5EFEB` - Velvety Cream):** Foundation surfaces that replace sterile digital white with linen and unbleached paper undertones.
- **Supporting Tones:**
  - **Warm Mocha (`#4A2E1B`):** Secondary container fills, inactive tab outlines, and card borders.
  - **Soft Charcoal (`#1F1C1A`):** High-legibility text color, preventing optical vibration against warm surfaces.
  - **Muted Parchment (`#E8DFD5`):** Subdued borders and divider rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally pairs editorial literary grace with technical precision:

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for bean titles, farm origins, roast profile names, and article headings. Conveys craft, vintage notebook documentation, and artisanal heritage.
- **Metric Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Employed for numeric data, grind settings, extraction times, water ratios, and functional microcopy. Its clear geometric construction guarantees immediate readability under dim brew-bar ambient conditions.
- **Label Tracking:** All uppercase metadata chips and numeric measurement specifiers utilize explicit positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for effortless scanability at small scales.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system implements an 8pt baseline rhythm inside an adaptive responsive frame:

- **Mobile Viewport (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Single-column card stacking prioritizes continuous one-handed thumb logging.
- **Tablet Viewport (768px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter-md: 1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates dual-pane layouts (log timeline on the left, sensory radar & parameters on the right).
- **Desktop Viewport (>1024px):** 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1200px` with `margin-lg: 3.5rem` (56px) and `gutter-lg: 2rem` (32px), creating balanced margins reminiscent of an open hardcover journal.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop-shadows in favor of tactile physical layering and warm ambient diffusion:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Canvas Base:** Soft velvety oat milk tint (`#FBF8F5`).
  - **Raised Card Layer:** Cream fill (`#F5EFEB`) framed with a subtle 1px border tinted in warm mocha at low opacity (`rgba(74, 46, 27, 0.12)`).
  - **Floating Active Layer:** Pure white/warm tint (`#FFFFFF`) with warm espresso ambient cast (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.04)`).
- **Micro-borders:** All interactive and grouped components rely on a distinct tactile 1px border (`#E8DFD5`) that mimics crisp letterpress indentations rather than floating flat graphics.

## Shapes

The geometry uses a balanced `Rounded` language (`8px` base, `16px` on `rounded-lg`, and `24px` on `rounded-xl`). 

- Standard inputs, sensory chips, and metric indicators inherit an `8px` corner radius.
- Journal log entry cards, cupping summary containers, and recipe modules feature `16px` corners.
- Interactive circular metrics (bean rating stamps, timer rings, and button pills) use pill-shaped radii for physical touchstone feedback.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2C1810`, text `#FBF8F5`, radius `8px`, height `48px`. On hover, subtle tint lift with amber border glow.
- **Secondary (Action Accent):** Background `#D97736`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`. Used exclusively for active brew interactions (e.g., "Start Pour", "Log Extraction").
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `#4A2E1B`, hairline border `1px solid rgba(74, 46, 27, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Tasting Logs
- **Log Entry Card:** Base color `#F5EFEB`, border `1px solid #E8DFD5`, radius `16px`, internal padding `1.25rem`. Houses recipe variables in clean columns with serif origin titles.
- **Active Brew Card:** High-contrast elevation with `#2C1810` background and high-visibility `#FBF8F5` sans numerals for live countdowns.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tasting Note Chips:** Pill geometry, border `1px solid rgba(74, 46, 27, 0.15)`, background `#FBF8F5`. Text in `label-md` (`#1F1C1A`).
- **Processing Badges (Washed / Natural / Anaerobic):** Background `#6E8B68` at 12% tint, text `#3E5739`, border `1px solid rgba(110, 139, 104, 0.3)`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Metric Steppers & Inputs:** Height `44px`, background `#FBF8F5`, border `1px solid #E8DFD5`, radius `8px`, text `#1F1C1A`. Numeric labels displayed in tabular monospace-compatible weights. Focus state triggers a 1px border in `#D97736` without thick focus rings.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Specialized Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded `4px` (checkbox) or circular (radio), border `1.5px solid #4A2E1B`. Active fill `#2C1810` with `#FBF8F5` check glyph.
- **Coffee Bean Intensity Indicator:** A 5-point organic bean glyph meter. Inactive state: `#E8DFD5` outline. Active roast/rating state: `#2C1810` fill with warm caramel outline.
- **Ratio Ratio Dial:** A dual-parameter slider displaying dried dose (g) to yield (g) with real-time numeric ratio feedback (e.g., `1:16.5`) styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold.
